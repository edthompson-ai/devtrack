Eval scorecard

The checker against 48 labelled criteria. Every run is stored per prompt version and model, so a change is measured before it ships. Precision and recall treat fail as the positive class.

Labels are AI-generated with a two-pass independent agreement check. Ed specified the process; a third independent AI pass adjudicated the disagreements, which are listed for human review. On a 20-criterion sample the two passes agreed 85% of the time (Cohen's kappa 0.72). Every labeller is a Claude model, like the judge, so a high score shows the models agree, not that they're right. A person relabelling with scripts/relabel.py is the stronger check.

How to read it

Fail precision: when the checker says fail, how often a person agrees. Low precision wastes reviewers' time.

Fail recall: of the criteria that really fail, how many the checker caught. Low recall lets unbuilt work through, which is the costly error.

Unclear rate: how much is handed to a person. Some is healthy (performance and platform claims can't be read from a diff).
